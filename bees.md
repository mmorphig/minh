Custom genus meaning:<br>
periodapis: related to elements on the periodic table, ex: iron, gold.<br>
    bees here are all from iron as an entrypoint into the genus.<br>
    the ferrum (iron) species comes from industrapis and diapis.<br>
mixtallicapis: alloys of metals, ex: bronze, brass.<br>
    bees here are don't have one entrypoint and come from mutating periodapis together.<br>
